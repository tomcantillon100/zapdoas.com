Heatwave Classroom Micro:bit Project

Investigation

Hot classrooms can make it harder for students and teachers to feel comfortable while learning.

I am exploring how a Micro:bit could help measure the temperature in a classroom. A study found a link between classroom temperature and students' test scores. It looked at temperatures from 20°C to 25°C. This does not mean that one temperature is best for every classroom, but it shows why classroom temperature is worth investigating [1].

References

[1] Haverinen-Shaughnessy, U. and Shaughnessy, R. J. (2015). “Effects of Classroom Ventilation Rate and Temperature on Students’ Test Scores.” PLOS ONE, 10(8), e0136165.
