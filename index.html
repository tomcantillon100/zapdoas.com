Heatwave Classroom – Smart Environment Monitor

Investigation Design Create Conclusion

Investigation

My project is a smart classroom monitor that detects high temperatures. Classrooms above 25°C can make students uncomfortable and make it harder to concentrate. My system monitors the temperature and responds when it gets too high.

Design

The micro:bit temperature sensor is the input. When the temperature reaches the chosen threshold, the micro:bit signals a motor driver to turn on the fan.

Create

I placed a small fan on a support stand and aimed it at the micro:bit while testing. This helped me observe how the setup responded. I can add more details about my test steps and results here.

Conclusion

My project helps make classrooms more comfortable by detecting high temperatures and automatically turning on a fan.
