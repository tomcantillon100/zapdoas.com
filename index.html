Heatwave Classroom – Smart Environment Monitor

investigation

My project is a smart classroom monitor that detects high temperatures. Classrooms above 25°C can make students uncomfortable and make it harder to concentrate. My system will monitor the temperature and turn on a fan so people will cool down when the room gets too hot and if it is too cold a led will turn on and tell people to turn on the heating system.

Design

The input is the temperature sensor on the micro:bit. The output is the motor driver which contains the fan that receives a signal when it needs to turn on as temperatures fluctuate and fan will turn on if to warm. I have them send radio signals on the same channel so when the motor driver receives a certain number It will complete a certain action.

Create

I used a small fan and put it on a support stand and aimed it at the mircobit so when it activates it is directly affecting the micro bit so more accuracy is involved when testing

Conclusion

My project helps make classrooms more comfortable by detecting high temperatures and automatically turning on a fan.
