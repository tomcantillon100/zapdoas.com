Heatwave classroom micro bit project

Investigation

For my project I investigated the serious problem of heatwave classrooms which effects students and teachers in various ways. this problem has been existing for as long as studying has been around and how to manage the heat while trying to learn. solutions have been made such as smart heating systems that turn on heating or fans on to change the temperture and aim to make it optimal. I found out that the tempuraure of 21 to 23 degrees was omptimal for studying and concentration and i aimed to use that for my project.
